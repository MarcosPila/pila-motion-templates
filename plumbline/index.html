    About

    Made-up story
    For the template
    Sample copy · every name and number here is invented

    We started with one van, a level and a rule we still keep: measure twice, quote once, and never leave a site messier than we found it.

    2011The first job: a back porch on Mill Road that is still standing straight.
    14People on the crew, and every one of them has swung a hammer here.
    1-yearWorkmanship warranty on every job, written into the contract.

    Plumbline began when Ada Brandt, a site carpenter, got tired of watching homeowners get one price at the door and another at the end. She bought a van, printed her prices on the back of a business card and promised to stick to them.

    Fifteen years on, the van is a fleet of three and the business card is this website, but the promise hasn't moved. You get a drawing, a fixed price and the same faces on site every day.
