Reservations

Save us a seat.

Walk-ins are always welcome. We hold a few tables for bookings, and the big table seats twelve.

Do you have gluten-free options?

Yes: soups, bowls and a gluten-free loaf on weekends. Our kitchen uses flour, so we can't promise zero traces.

Can I order a cake?

Give us three days' notice. Call or ask at the counter.

Is there parking?

Street parking and the Macdonell lot, two minutes away.
