The bath

Bubbles, not stress.

Calm music, warm water, gentle shampoo, and as many breaks as your dog needs. Pop a bubble or two while you read.

  1. Brush out

    Tangles and loose undercoat come out first.

  2. Warm bath

    Oatmeal shampoo, or hypoallergenic for sensitive skin.

  3. Blow-dry

    Low and quiet, with a towel break for nervous pups.

  4. Trim & tidy

    Haircut, nails, ears and paw pads.

  5. Bandana & a treat

    Plus a photo for you when they're ready.

Do you use cages?

Never. Dogs wait in a gated lounge with a bed and water, or with us.

What if my dog is nervous?

Book the quiet first slot of the day. We go slowly, take breaks, and stop if it's too much.

Do you need vaccine records?

Yes: rabies and DHPP, from your vet, before the first visit.
