Can you roof in winter?

Yes, down to about −10 °C with cold-weather shingles and hand-sealing. Repairs happen year-round.

Do you deal with insurance?

We write hail and wind reports with photos and meet the adjuster on the roof if you'd like.

How long does a roof last?

Architectural shingles usually give 25 to 30 years here, if the attic is vented and the eaves are protected from ice.
