The route

Six stops, one season.

  1. 01 · Day 1

    Vancouver

    Meet the group, dinner on Granville Island, early night.

  2. 02 · Days 2–4

    Tofino

    Rainforest walks, storm watching and a float-plane hop.

  3. 03 · Days 5–7

    Haida Gwaii

    Totem villages with Haida guides, by boat and on foot.

  4. 04 · Days 8–10

    Whitehorse

    Hot springs by day, northern lights by night.

  5. 05 · Days 11–12

    Jasper

    Icefields Parkway, Maligne Canyon, dark-sky stargazing.

  6. 06 · Days 13–14

    Banff

    Lake Louise at sunrise, a farewell dinner in the mountains.

About Northbound

A guide who stayed.

Leah Tremblay came to Whitehorse for one summer of guiding in 2015 and never left. She started Northbound to run the kind of trip she wanted to take: small, slow and led by people who live there.

Every guide on our team lives on the route. Half our trips are led with First Nations partners, who set the pace on their own land.

“We saw the lights three nights out of four. Leah knew exactly where to drive.”Martin & Josée, Northern Lights 2025
