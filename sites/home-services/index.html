FAQ

Good questions.

Do you charge to come out?

The $129 service call covers the trip and the first 30 minutes. Club members pay no call-out fee.

Will the price change once you start?

No. You get one written price before we start. If we find something new, we stop and ask first.

What areas do you cover?

Hamilton, Burlington, Ancaster, Dundas, Stoney Creek and Grimsby.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Our electricians are licensed through the ESA, our gas fitters through TSSA, and we carry $5 million in liability insurance.
