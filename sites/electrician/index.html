Questions

Good to know.

Do you pull permits?

Yes, every time the code needs one. We book the ESA inspection and send you the certificate when it passes.

How do your prices work?

You get a fixed price after we see the job, before any work starts. If we find something unexpected, we stop and talk to you first.

Do you take emergency calls?

For no-power, sparking or burning-smell calls, phone us and we'll tell you honestly how soon we can be there. If you smell burning, turn off the main breaker first.

Is your work guaranteed?

Our workmanship is guaranteed for five years. Fixtures and chargers carry their maker's warranty, and we handle the claim for you.
