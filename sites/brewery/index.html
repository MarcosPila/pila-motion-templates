The brewery

Brewed by the harbour.

Moira started Cold Snap in a garage in 2017 with a ten-gallon kettle and a lot of opinions about pilsner. Now it's a twenty-barrel brewhouse, a taproom with twelve lines, and the same opinions.

Malt from Nova Scotia farms when we can get it, hops from New Brunswick, and water straight from the city tap.

Can I bring the kids?

Yes, until 8 pm, in the taproom and on the patio. We have root beer on tap for them.

Are dogs allowed?

On the patio, always. There's a water bowl by the door and biscuits behind the bar.

Do you fill growlers?

Any clean growler, any tap except the casks. Glass ones are for sale at the bar too.

Is there food?

Food trucks Thursday to Sunday, pretzels and pickles every day, and you can bring your own.
