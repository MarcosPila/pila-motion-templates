How do I look after it?

Wash gently twice a day, a thin layer of the balm we give you, no swimming or sun for two weeks.

Do you take deposits?

Yes, $80, which comes off the final price. It holds your date and pays for the drawing time.

Can you cover an old tattoo?

Often, yes. Book a consult with Kofi or Jun and bring a clear photo of the old piece.
