Do I need a health card?

Yes, a valid Nova Scotia health card. Visitors from other provinces can be seen; we'll bill your home province.

Can I get a virtual visit?

Yes, by phone or video for many concerns, like prescription renewals and test results.

How do I get my test results?

Through the patient portal, usually within three business days. We call you if anything needs attention.

Is parking free?

Yes, with accessible spots by the main door.
